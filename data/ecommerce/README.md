# E-Commerce Dashboard Sample Datasets

This directory contains standalone, production-ready sample CSV files designed for the **E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard** (`/dashboard/ecommerce`).

> **Note**: These files are placed here for offline analysis, data warehouse ingestion (PostgreSQL/Supabase/Snowflake), reporting, and testing. They are **not** wired into the live Next.js application runtime.

---

## Files Overview

| File Name | Rows | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| [`ecommerce_orders.csv`](./ecommerce_orders.csv) | 379+ | Comprehensive header-level orders table containing order IDs, dates, customer info, fulfillment/payment statuses, item counts, taxes, and order totals. Matches the **KPI Total Orders (379)** and **Recent Orders Table**. |
| [`ecommerce_order_items.csv`](./ecommerce_order_items.csv) | 1,000+ | Detailed order line items linking `order_id` to `stock_code` with unit prices, quantities, and line totals for basket analysis. |
| [`ecommerce_products.csv`](./ecommerce_products.csv) | 15 | Product catalog including top revenue-generating SKUs (`85123A`, `71053`, `84406B`, `22752`, `21733`), revenue shares, and units sold. Powers the **Top Products** widget. |
| [`ecommerce_customers.csv`](./ecommerce_customers.csv) | 35 | VIP customer accounts and buyers with lifetime spend, total orders, country, and VIP tiers. Powers the **Top VIP Buyers** widget. |
| [`ecommerce_country_breakdown.csv`](./ecommerce_country_breakdown.csv) | 10 | Geographic sales performance by market (UK, Germany, France, EIRE, Spain, etc.) with sales revenue, order counts, and market share. Powers the **Geographic Sales** widget. |
| [`ecommerce_monthly_revenue.csv`](./ecommerce_monthly_revenue.csv) | 12 | 12-month aggregated revenue and order counts (Jan - Dec 2026) powering the **Revenue Overview Area/Bar Chart**. |

---

## Schema Reference

### 1. `ecommerce_orders.csv`
- `order_id` *(string)*: Unique invoice / order number (e.g., `536365` or cancellation prefix `C536379`).
- `order_date` *(timestamp)*: ISO formatted timestamp (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss`).
- `customer_id` *(string)*: Unique identifier of the purchasing customer.
- `customer_name` *(string)*: Customer name.
- `country` *(string)*: Country of destination.
- `order_status` *(string)*: `Completed`, `Returned`, or `Pending`.
- `payment_status` *(string)*: `Paid`, `Refunded`, or `Pending`.
- `fulfillment_status` *(string)*: `Fulfilled`, `Returned`, or `Unfulfilled`.
- `items_count` *(integer)*: Number of items included in the order.
- `subtotal` *(decimal)*: Subtotal amount before taxes & shipping.
- `tax_amount` *(decimal)*: Applicable VAT / sales tax.
- `shipping_fee` *(decimal)*: Delivery fee (0.00 for orders over $100).
- `total_amount` *(decimal)*: Final charged amount.

### 2. `ecommerce_products.csv`
- `stock_code` *(string)*: SKU / Product code (e.g., `85123A`).
- `product_name` *(string)*: Product title.
- `category` *(string)*: Product classification category.
- `unit_price` *(decimal)*: Retail list price.
- `units_sold` *(integer)*: Total units sold over the tracking period.
- `total_revenue` *(decimal)*: Gross revenue generated by the SKU.
- `revenue_share` *(string)*: Percentage contribution to total store sales.

### 3. `ecommerce_customers.csv`
- `customer_id` *(string)*: Customer account ID.
- `customer_name` *(string)*: Full customer name / company.
- `email` *(string)*: Customer email address.
- `country` *(string)*: Customer country.
- `total_orders` *(integer)*: Lifetime order count.
- `total_spend` *(decimal)*: Lifetime monetary value (LTV).
- `vip_tier` *(string)*: VIP loyalty classification (`Platinum`, `Gold`, `Silver`, `Bronze`).

### 4. `ecommerce_monthly_revenue.csv`
- `year` *(integer)*: 2026.
- `month_num` *(integer)*: 1 to 12.
- `period` *(string)*: Three-letter month abbreviation (`Jan` - `Dec`).
- `revenue` *(decimal)*: Gross sales revenue for the month.
- `orders_count` *(integer)*: Total orders placed in the month.
- `avg_order_value` *(decimal)*: Average order value (AOV).

### 5. `ecommerce_country_breakdown.csv`
- `country` *(string)*: Geographic market name.
- `total_sales` *(decimal)*: Cumulative revenue from the country.
- `orders_count` *(integer)*: Total orders placed from the country.
- `market_share_percentage` *(string)*: Percentage of total store sales.
